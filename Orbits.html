Orbits

A music composition toolkit that is designed for cosmos-themed ambient/neo-classical/cinematic music.

In this toolkit, you'll find sounds that indicate every major planet in our solar system, as well as other cosmic elements and celestial bodies such as the M87 Galaxy's blackhole.
